SINCE 2001

25 Years of Aerial Excellence

Founded in 2001 by veteran aeronaut Arthur Pendelton, AEROVA was born out of a deep reverence for peaceful flight and airborne hospitality.

What started as a single balloon drifting over misty morning valleys has grown into California's premier hot air balloon fleet. We hold a 100% safety record across more than 15,000 completed flights.
