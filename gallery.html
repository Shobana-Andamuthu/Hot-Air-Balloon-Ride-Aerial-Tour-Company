Pilot Aerial Photography Tips

Planning to bring your own DSLR or smartphone? Here is how to capture stunning shots:

  • 1. Golden Hour Light: Golden light is softest during the first 30 minutes after lift-off.
  • 2. Wide Angle Lenses: Use 16-24mm focal lengths to capture both the balloon burner & landscape.
  • 3. Secure Wrist Straps: Always use neck or wrist straps when holding cameras over the basket rail.
